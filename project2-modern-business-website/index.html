Welcome to Bun Intended

We're a small burger restaurant on Götgatan in Södermalm. We smash our beef fresh on a very hot grill, bake our buns every morning and make our sauces from scratch.

Come with friends after work, bring a date or celebrate something big. You'll get table service, local craft beer and a menu that's a little bit cheesy, in the best way.
