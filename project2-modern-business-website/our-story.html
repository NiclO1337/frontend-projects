Our Story

Bun Intended started with two friends, Elin and Jonas, and one strong opinion: burgers deserve better ingredients, and puns deserve a restaurant.

They met in kitchens around Stockholm and spent their evenings arguing about the perfect smash burger: the right beef, the right heat, the right bun. In 2016 they stopped arguing and bought a food truck.

From food truck to Götgatan

  1. 2016

    The food truck

    We buy a second-hand truck and start smashing burgers at street-food markets around Stockholm. The menu has three items and the queue is long.

  2. 2018

    Street-food champions

    The Classic Pun wins a local street-food competition. Suddenly everyone wants to know who is behind the pickles.

  3. 2020

    Götgatan

    We open our own restaurant on Götgatan, with a proper kitchen, table service and a seat for everyone in the queue.

  4. 2024

    New look, new bar

    We renovate and add a bar with craft beer from local breweries.

  5. Today

    Still smashing, still punning

    Same recipe, same grill, same questionable jokes. Come and say hi.

What we stand for

Swedish beef from local farms

Our beef comes from farms close to Stockholm. It is ground fresh and smashed to order.

Buns baked fresh every morning

Every bun is baked in the morning and served the same day. Never frozen, never rushed.

Puns served free of charge

The burgers cost money. The puns are on the house, whether you ask for them or not.
